# Utility Roadmap — 2026-07-16

46 ideas · 5 lenses (listing agent, buyer agent, unsurfaced compute, competitor gaps,
retention loops) · judged + code-verified. Goal: FREE→Pro conversion + weekly retention.

## Verdict
The engine is dramatically ahead of the UI: the highest-value work is serialization and rendering, not modeling. Five independent lenses converged on surfacing the prod-shipped price-cut classifier, four on the equity/prior-sale story, and four on watch/refresh retention loops — that convergence is the roadmap. Verified in code: original_list_price, appearance_tier, hygiene_note, feed_dom, and saleHistory are already on the wire with zero readers, while predict_cut_probability, predict_dom, and recommend_price_for_target_dom are fully built but only called from the legacy cma.py path — one engine-gate emit (the CMA_COMP_SCORE_SURFACE pattern already exists) unlocks all three. The screen/PDF DOM disagreement (synthetic elasticity in pricing-strategy.ts vs model-backed _dom_pricing_band in the PDF) is closer to a credibility bug than a feature and should be fixed first. The one genuine big swing is the retention platform (cron + Resend on the existing mailer): it converts compbird from an appointment-day tool into a weekly habit, and it's what makes cancelling feel like losing coverage. Sequence the pricing-model surfacing BEFORE the backlogged interactive pricing-rail drag — those models are the drag's honest backend.

## Features (ranked by utility-per-effort)

Show the calibrated cut probability at each pricing-strategy price: 'List at $475k → 61% chance you cut; at $450k → 24%.' Replaces the boolean cutRisk flag with the model no competitor (RPR, Cloud CMA, BoldTrail) ships. The single strongest signal in the entire idea pool.

### Real DOM model behind the pricing panel (PDF parity) + Overpricing Cost readout  `[small · Pro · 2 votes]`

## Big swings
### Retention Platform: Property Watch + Home Pulse + Farm Digest
One Railway cron worker + Resend on the existing src/lib/mailer.ts unlocks the entire push cluster: star-a-property watches ('2 new comps sold near 123 Main — estimate moved $455k → $462k', re-run WITH the agent's persisted tuning), Homebeat-style monthly seller reports to enrolled past clients, and a weekly farm/market digest with cut alerts from the classifier.
*Why worth it:* Twelve of 46 ideas across four lenses are variants of this one platform — the highest vote mass in the pool. It converts compbird from a pull-only appointment tool into a Monday-morning habit, and cancelling means losing coverage of your farm and your book of past clients — the strongest churn defense a $20/mo product can build. Homebeat proves agents pay for exactly this, and compbird's no-per-seat-MLS-license position means every send legally re-runs the full engine with signals (cut probability, heat) competitors structurally can't include. Charge the fixed infra cost to Property Watch; every subsequent loop (farm digest, portfolio re-run, Home Pulse) is then small.

### Present Mode — live listing-appointment walkthrough
Full-screen slideshow of an existing report (value reveal → comps with match scores → market heat → pricing strategy) with the what-if editor and comp tuning live, so the agent toggles a renovation or pins a comp and the value recomputes in front of the seller.
*Why worth it:* Cloud CMA Live and BoldTrail Present made interactive presentation table-stakes for winning listings, but theirs re-sort slides while compbird's engine actually re-values in real time — a visible, demo-able wow moment that sells Pro in one appointment. It's a view-layer recomposition of already-shipped components (report page, what-if editor, tuning persistence, pricing panel), so it's a medium-effort swing with no new backend, and it becomes the anchor of every demo video and social clip.

### Reality Scoreboard — your estimates vs what actually happened
When a property the agent priced later lists or sells, detect it via the hourly pool and email 'you said $465k in April; it just listed at $479k', accumulating a personal accuracy card ('your estimates land within 4.1% of sale price').
*Why worth it:* This is the emotional lock-in play: the product periodically proves the agent right, and the accuracy card becomes listing-presentation ammo they lose by cancelling. It also dovetails with backlog proposal #9 (published accuracy figure) — per-agent accuracy is the personalized version of the same trust asset. Requires the retention platform's cron plus a report-history registry (the server-side saved-reports big-bet), so it's phase 2 of the platform, not a standalone build.

- Offer & List-Price Stress Test (drag interaction) — duplicates the backlogged interactive pricing-rail drag; its models ship as the Price-Cut Risk Gauge + DOM parity, which become that big-bet's backend.
- Living reports 'what changed' diff — folded into the server-side saved-reports big-bet rather than a separate feature; it is that bet's killer detail, not its own build.
- Overpricing Cost Simulator — merged into Price-Cut Gauge + DOM parity; it is the same two models composed at an arbitrary price, not a distinct feature.
- Value Drift Badges + Stale-CMA freshness badges + Listing Watch — all subsumed by Property Watch in the retention platform (4 lens variants of one loop).
- Monthly Seller Update Report — merged with Home Pulse (identical idea, two lenses).
- Bidding-War Barometer + price-cut share — merged into one Market Heat metrics feature (same DuckDB query).
- Monthly agent recap ('you priced 14 homes') — vanity aggregation with weak retention pull; UsageEvent data isn't going anywhere if it earns a slot later.
- Curbside mode — this is responsive-design QA on the existing flow, not a feature; do it as polish, not roadmap.
- ARV Mode — niche flipper reframe with no rent data behind it; revisit if investor-mode batch shows traction.
- Off-Market Sales Layer — the 333k recorded sales lack condition/spec quality and the regional test showed supplemental data only wins in MLS-thin areas; risky to present as a comp set. Revisit post-expansion.
- Seller Cost-Basis Card — merged into Equity Snapshot (same prior-sale data, buyer-side framing becomes a toggle).
