## Layout & grid discipline
**Overall:** Compbird's layout system is fundamentally sound and recently improved: one 6xl rail concept, a real deep-ink band per page, numbered report zones with hairline dividers, and the new sticky toolbar genuinely stitches the ~5-viewport report together (address/estimate/jump chips stay put, scroll-mt offsets are correct). The discipline gaps are one level down: the site quietly runs THREE different left edges on the landing page because three container recipes coexist (padding-outside-max-w sections at 384px, padding-inside nav/trust-strip/studio at 416px, and wide slabs whose inner content lands at 368px); the report's flagship data table is an uncontrolled auto-layout table that scatters its columns across the full rail with a dead gutter after Address; and two-column grids (report ZONE 1, the analytics two-up, FinalCTA) repeatedly pair a tall column with a short one and let the void show. All of these are small, mechanical fixes — container recipe unification and column-width/row-spanning decisions — not redesigns.

## Visual appearance page-by-page — design-director critique of the live site post dark-band-rhythm and featured-Pro-card changes

**Overall:** The 2026-07-16 wave is broadly wrap-safe — flex-wrap idioms, min-w-0 truncation, and scroll-mt offsets are used consistently, and the explicit question in this lens has a clean answer: the sticky report toolbar (report-view.tsx:242, fixed top-16 z-30) does NOT collide with the studio header on small screens, because the /comps header is a single non-wrapping flex row locked to h-16 at every breakpoint (comps/page.tsx:62) and the toolbar's show/hide sentinel uses a matching -64px rootMargin (report-view.tsx:497); the account dropdown sits above it at z-50 and Leaflet is isolated.
