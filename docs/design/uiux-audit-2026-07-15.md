## Executive summary
Compbird's analysis core is genuinely ahead of genre — live comp tuning, what-if overrides, method convergence, honest confidence tiers — but the product around it is a memoryless single-tab calculator whose answer evaporates, wrapped in a funnel that drops visitor intent at the auth wall, on a landing page whose visual rhythm collapsed when the light flip silently turned both dark instrument bands into invisible white slabs. Six lenses converged hard on three failures, and they are the three levers. Lever 1, REPORT LIFECYCLE: a live report has no URL (verified: comp-studio.tsx:359/684 never writes it, though inbound ?address= deep links already work), tuned work and PDFs die with the tab, and history is 10 localStorage entries — so the weekly-return loop that retains a $20/mo subscriber has no return leg; the MVP fix is ~20 lines because the plumbing exists. Lever 2, INTENT PLUMBING: three divergent safeRedirect copies plus a query-stripping proxy mean demo intent, address intent, Pro-purchase intent, and even the already-'fixed' /portfolio destination (verified still broken at auth.ts:63) all silently reset to /comps — one shared sanitizer + forwarding the query fixes five findings at once and unlocks the single best growth move, an address input in the hero that auto-prices the visitor's own listing as their first post-signup screen. Lever 3, DEPTH EXPOSURE: the PDF deliverable is four viewports deep, Portfolio is hidden in an avatar dropdown while marketing promotes it top-level, analytics arrive as unlabeled gray slabs that look like unloaded placeholders, and FREE users get six competing paid CTAs instead of one confident ask. Fix these three and both stated goals move: utilization (agents see, save, and return to the depth they're paying for) and flow (no journey silently loses what the user asked for). The queued proposals are real but skewed cosmetic; #7, #4, #1, and #2 earned multi-lens endorsement and should be promoted — #7 first, since it is the only queued item that repairs a funnel break.

## Lens verdicts
- **Visual hierarchy & first impression**: Copy system is strong but the light flip flattened eight sections into one undifferentiated paper surface; the hero never names the category or audience, and the biggest tile on the features grid is two-thirds empty.
- **Conversion flow & funnel**: Honest bones (sample-first, no-card free tier) but visitor intent does not survive the auth wall in any form — demo, address, Pro, and portfolio destinations all die in three divergent redirect sanitizers, and there is zero re-engagement capture for the out-of-coverage majority.
- **Studio UX & product utilization**: An unusually capable v1 instrument that rewards the 2-second free estimate and hides everything justifying $20/mo — PDF at the bottom, Portfolio behind an avatar menu, and nothing an agent builds survives a reload.
- **Pure visual design**: A real, disciplined identity being starved: one blue on paper-white does every job across 22 frames, the signature aerial motif appears exactly once, and the paid report's evidence graphics read as unloaded skeletons and unstyled range sliders.
- **Information architecture & navigation**: Small, mostly coherent IA whose app layer is invisible and stateless — reports have no URL, two nav labels (Portfolio, Market reports) promise destinations that don't deliver, and the marketing nav is auth-blind.
- **Genre expectations (working agent)**: Beats RPR/Cloud CMA at the analysis layer but misses the genre's core mental model — 'a CMA is a document I make, keep, and send'; no saved reports, no re-download, no share path, no comps export.

## Themes
### Report lifecycle & persistence  `[high impact / medium effort / 6 findings]`
**Problem:** A live report exists only in component state: no URL (back/refresh/bookmark/share all discard it — verified at comp-studio.tsx:359,684), tuning work (excludes, pins, what-ifs, summary text) is lost on reload, rendered PDFs are orphaned unguessable tokens, and history is 10 localStorage recents. Three lenses independently flagged this as the top gap; it is the missing return leg of the weekly-use loop that retains Pro subscribers.
**Recommendation:** Phase 1 (~20 lines): on select() success, history.replaceState the resolved ?parcelId=&address= (planDeepLink already no-ops on the mounted subject, so no loop), and render recents chips + Cmd-K rows as real <a> elements so ctrl-click/two-tab compare works. Phase 2: persist per-subject tuning to localStorage keyed by parcel_id and rehydrate on re-pick (the /preview wire already accepts excluded/forced/subjectOverrides). Phase 3: a Report Prisma row (accountId, address, parcelId, estimate band, confidenceTier, pdfToken, createdAt) written on resolve and updated on PDF render — the PortfolioRun pattern already establishes this — surfaced as a 'Reports' list on /account with re-open and re-download.

### Redirect & intent plumbing (one root cause, five breaks)  `[high impact / medium effort / 8 findings]`
**Problem:** Three divergent safeRedirect sanitizers plus a query-stripping proxy silently reset user intent: the post-auth /portfolio fix shipped in the design log is INCOMPLETE (verified — auth.ts:63 regex admits only /comps, so nav Portfolio → join → lands in /comps), signup validation errors drop the redirect param entirely, /account can't round-trip re-auth, proxy.ts:21 rebuilds the redirect from pathname only so ?demo=1 and any future ?address= physically cannot survive, and pricing-page Pro intent lands users in the studio with checkout hidden in a menu.
**Recommendation:** Extract ONE shared safeRedirect helper used by join/page.tsx, signin/page.tsx, and actions/auth.ts (this allowlist has now caused two funnel bugs); admit /portfolio and /account plus a vetted query whitelist (demo, address, parcelId, intent); make proxy.ts forward the original query; include the sanitized redirect in the error-bounce querystring. Then cash the unlocked wins: carry ?intent=pro through join to a one-click 'Finish upgrading' banner (startSubscription exists), and add an address input to the hero that rides ?address= through signup so the user's first authenticated screen is their own listing priced — the strongest completion motive available.

### Deliverable & depth exposure inside the report  `[high impact / medium effort / 8 findings]`
**Problem:** The report is a ~5-viewport same-weight river: the PDF — the product's declared payoff ('Three moves from address to PDF') — is one button at 90% depth (2 lenses, both high severity); analytics and market sections are findable only by scrolling; the occasional-use what-if editor claims the primary above-the-fold column on every lookup; the comps table lacks the subject reference row every appraisal grid leads with; map pins carry address-only tooltips; and there is no comps CSV despite the portfolio already shipping one.
**Recommendation:** Add a slim sticky report toolbar after ZONE 1 scrolls off (address · estimate · confidence pill · Download PDF · section jump chips — the anchor plumbing already exists via COMPS_SECTION_ID); collapse the what-if editor to a one-line affordance, auto-expanded when overrides are active; number the report sections with the brand's mono numerals; pin a tinted Subject row atop CompsTable; enrich pin tooltips with price+match (one line in mapPoints); add 'Download comps (CSV)' reusing portfolio csv.ts.

### Pro feature routing & discoverability  `[high impact / small effort / 9 findings]`
**Problem:** The two features that justify $20/mo are systematically under-routed: Portfolio is invisible from the studio (avatar-dropdown only — flagged independently by THREE lenses) while marketing promotes it top-level to a signup wall that never explains it; 'Market reports' is a nav promise pointing at non-clickable cards with no product page; the footer omits Portfolio; below sm, /portfolio has no labeled route back to the studio; the marketing nav is auth-blind so signed-in users appear logged out.
**Recommendation:** Ship the one-line fixes now: 'Portfolio' link in the /comps header (mirroring portfolio's own 'Comp studio' link), 'Comp studio' as first item in the avatar dropdown, Portfolio in the footer, avatar letter from email not plan. Next session: a short portfolio explainer band/tile on the landing anchored from the nav, and a session-aware marketing nav ('Open studio' when the cookie exists). Longer term the market-engine plan (M0-M2) should claim /markets as a real route; until then make market cards deep-link into the studio.

### Landing rhythm & evidence graphics  `[medium impact / medium effort / 14 findings]`
**Problem:** The light flip left .cb-dark byte-identical to the paper palette (verified compbird.css:112), so both instrument bands render as invisible white slabs and eight sections share one surface, one rail, one h2 scale; the features bento's dominant 2x2 tile is two-thirds void; the formless blurred glow blob floats on every page; the flagship accuracy visual reads as five unstyled range sliders and draws markers for methods that returned null; the $/sqft comp bars in the PAID report read as unloaded gray placeholders (2 lenses); the signature aerial motif appears once while the report ships a default full-color OSM map.
**Recommendation:** Treat queued #1 as the page's rhythm system: give .cb-dark a real deep-ink palette applied to exactly ONE band per page, --cb-tint header bands elsewhere. Rebuild PpsfBars (baseline near min−5%, median rule, tinted bars) and redraw accuracy as a true convergence plot on a shared price axis (ghost rows for skipped methods) — build once, reuse for the studio's Method Convergence. Fill the bento lead tile with AerialMap/comp-stack evidence; emit the glow ring only where a panel crops it; CSS-filter the Leaflet tiles to survey-linework gray with ember/slate pins.

### Conversion surface tuning  `[high impact / small effort / 7 findings]`
**Problem:** A FREE user's first live report — the highest-traffic conversion surface in the product — stacks six competing Stripe entry points (2 lenses endorse queued #4); the landing arc skips price entirely (the free-first offer, the strongest de-risking fact, lives only in 0.875rem microcopy); both pricing CTAs open with the identical verb 'Start free'; the nav carries two same-rank pill CTAs; and at the final ask the sample-report path is at its faintest exactly where demo intent peaks.
**Recommendation:** Implement queued #4 (one locked evidence band, one unlock CTA, header Upgrade as the only other entry) and queued #2 plus CTA differentiation ('Unlock the evidence — $20/mo' on Pro); add one slim $0/$20 beat between Coverage and FinalCTA reusing the pricing page's headline; demote 'Sign up free' to a text link; give FinalCTA's sample link the hero's bordered-pill treatment plus 'free account, no card' microcopy.

### Coverage honesty & re-engagement capture  `[high impact / small effort / 5 findings]`
**Problem:** Most US visitors are out of coverage, and the product never tells them: no-results says only 'No matches', /join never mentions VA+DC, first-run says 'any Virginia address' (DC missing), the waitlist component was deleted with a stale comment claiming otherwise — so ad-driven out-of-state agents complete signup, hit a bare dead end, and leave no email. Compounding trust: the typeahead prints impossible geography ('Blacksburg County').
**Recommendation:** One line in the no-results panel ('compbird covers Virginia + D.C. today') plus a one-field 'Tell me when you reach my state' capture rebuilt behind the existing rate-limited endpoint pattern; echo coverage on /join; fix first-run and placeholder copy; sanitize the search index county field against the known jurisdiction list. This converts wasted ad spend into the launch list for the planned US expansion.

### Small-friction sweep (flow polish)  `[medium impact / small effort / 11 findings]`
**Problem:** A cluster of independently-small momentum leaks: FirstRun's 'Start pricing' button starts nothing (focus returns to body); portfolio rows fire window.open on any click including text selection AND spawn a tab per row with no way back to the run; the sample report refuses the tuning the onboarding just promised; portfolio's Confidence column has no explainer; no computed-at timestamp behind the 'refreshed hourly' claim; legal pages unreachable from the app; no sitemap/robots.
**Recommendation:** Batch these into one polish session: focus the search input on FirstRun dismiss (the combobox selector already exists); implement queued #5 and switch portfolio rows to same-tab ?address= deep links with a 'Back to portfolio run' breadcrumb; add the sample-is-read-only hint; reuse ConfidenceBadge in the portfolio table; append computedAt to DataFreshness with a stale re-run chip; link Terms/Privacy from the studio disclaimer; add sitemap.ts + robots.ts (~15 lines).
